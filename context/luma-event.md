## About Event

# MakerMods Physical AI Hacks 🤖

This is the best Physical AI hackathon you've been looking for.

Join MakerMods, Dream Labs, and LiveKit on November 7–8 at Founders Inc. in San Francisco for a weekend of building physical AI: robots that act on the world, agents that coordinate those actions, and applications that bring the two together.

We're bringing bimanual 6-axis robot arms, Dream Labs' Dream-1, LiveKit's real-time infrastructure, and a target of 200 builders into one space. Bring your ideas, your teammates, and a willingness to debug something that can actually move.

Brought to you by:

### MakerMods
MakerMods is a full-stack robotics company making Physical AI accessible to everyone. We build affordable robot hardware and software that lets people teach robots to do physical tasks—from makers experimenting at home to researchers building the next generation of robot intelligence. Built for makers, by makers.

### Dream Labs
Dream Labs is bringing "everyday capabilities to everyday robots." Its robot brain, Dream-1, is built to work across different robots, tasks, and environments. Through the Dream API, developers can turn camera observations and task instructions into robot actions, and adapt the model with their own demonstrations.

### LiveKit
LiveKit is building the real-time layer for Physical AI. Their global edge network enables robots to be connected to human operators and models in the cloud across the world with ultra low latency. LiveKit also brings the platform for Voice AI, enabling easy to use voice AI agents for controlling your robot.

## The Challenge: Two ways to build

### 🏁 Hill Climb (OUR TRACK)

One challenge. One leaderboard.

Take on a shared, long horizon Physical AI tasks using our Metal Arm stations.

Your team's rank is based only on your eval score.

No pitching. No larping. It's all skill based.

You'll collect demonstrations and fine-tune the latest SOTA World Action Model by Dream Labs: Dream-1. Then your team builds the harness around the model: deciding what happens next, checking whether an action worked, and recovering when it didn't.

The same Dream-1 model can produce very different results depending on the fine-tuning data and the harness you build around it.

Can your robot finish the task? Can it recover from a failed attempt? Can you push your score higher before the deadline?

Scores come from task performance against published rules. Leading teams will make scored attempts live on stage while the competition continues at the other stations.

The full challenge and scoring rules will be shared before the event.

### 💡 Creative Applications (Track 2)
What would you build if your agent had robot arms? Build an application around Dream-1 and our Maker Arm stations. Connect robot actions to a LiveKit Voice Agent, human input, and everyday tools. Directions: voice assistant that asks for help when stuck; robot workflow triggered by calendar/message; interactive people+robot experience; a physical tool a software agent can call. Book robot time, bring props, film the project. Three finalists pitch on stage with demo videos; judging panel picks 1st/2nd/3rd. Creative awards judged separately from the hill-climb leaderboard, rubric published beforehand.

## Who should join?
Robot-learning researchers. ML engineers. Agent builders. Hardware hackers. Students. Designers. Teams need: collecting demonstrations, building agents, integrating tools, designing interactions, making the whole stack work. Workshops from MakerMods, Dream Labs and more sponsors.
